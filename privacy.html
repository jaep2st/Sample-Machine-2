Privacy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Your music never leaves your device. All chopping, tempo and key detection happens in your browser.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email.
  • No uploads: the audio you load is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts.

What stays on your device

Your favorites (the hearts you tap) are saved in your browser's local storage so they're still there next time. Clearing your browser data removes them. Nothing else is stored.

Services the page loads from

Like any website, loading the page means a few services see a normal web request from your device (including your IP address):

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts provides the typeface.
  • jsDelivr delivers the optional Essentia beat finder.

These services handle that data under their own privacy policies. They do not receive your audio.
