Privacy

Last updated November 28, 2026

Your music never leaves your device. All chopping, tempo and key detection happens in your browser.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts and no sign-up to use the free version.
  • No uploads: the audio you load is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • No advertising or tracking scripts.

What stays on your device

Your favorites, saved beat sessions, today's free save count and, if you buy Pro, your license key are stored in your browser on this device so they're still there next time. Clearing your browser data removes them.

If you buy Pro

Payments are handled by Polar, our merchant of record. Polar collects your name, email and payment details to process the purchase, send receipts and handle tax, under Polar's privacy policy. We see your email address and order details so we can help with support and refunds. We never see your full card number.

To check that a license key is valid, the app sends the key (and a device label) to Polar now and then. No audio or usage data is sent.

Services the page uses

Like any website, loading the page means a few services see a normal web request from your device (including your IP address):

  • Cloudflare hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts provides the typeface.
  • Polar checks Pro license keys and runs checkout.

These services handle that data under their own privacy policies. They do not receive your audio.
