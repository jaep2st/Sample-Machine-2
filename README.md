# Sample Machine 2

Chop any track into beat-snapped loops and one-shots, right in your browser.

**Live:** https://jaep2st.github.io/Sample-Machine-2/

- Finds tempo, key and downbeats, then cuts 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16-bar chops on real bar lines
- Every loop is conformed to the exact length at a whole-number BPM, with a seamless loop point
- Saved WAVs carry their tempo, beat count and key, so DAWs can sync them on drop
- Click track, Set beat 1, Tap tempo and one-beat nudges to fix anything by ear
- Pitch, tempo, reverse and 8 styles per chop; pads that loop or play once (keys 1–9 on a computer)
- Flip modes built on legendary techniques: Soul Speed-Up, Screwed, Pitch-Down, Micro-Chop Flip, Off-Grid Flip, Stab Kit, Drum Kit, Filtered Bass, SP Grit, Dusty Loop
- Breaks tab finds drum-only sections; Open tab finds melodic sections with few drums
- Real low-pass and high-pass filters, SP-1200-style grit and vinyl
- Your audio never leaves your device

The optional second beat finder uses [Essentia.js](https://essentia.upf.edu/) (AGPL-3.0). Set `USE_ESSENTIA = false` in `index.html` to ship without it.
