Sample Machine 2 is a free tool that chops audio into loops and one-shots inside your web browser. By using it, you agree to these terms.

Your music, your responsibility

Only load audio you have the right to use. Chopping a song with this tool does not give you any rights to that song. If you release music that uses a sample of someone else's recording or composition, it's your job to get permission (clear the sample) from the people who own it.

What the tool does with your audio

Your audio is processed on your own device. It is not uploaded to us or stored by us. See the privacy page for details.

Results can be wrong

Tempo, key and beat detection are automatic estimates and can be wrong. Check your loops by ear before you use them. The click track and the Set beat 1 and Tap tempo controls are there to help you correct them.
